1Overview

Gobiz ("Gobiz," "we," "us," or "our") is a technology partner combining software engineering, AI, DevOps, cloud infrastructure, and technical leadership to build and scale digital products. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent data practices regarding how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website, engage with our services, submit inquiries, or collaborate with us as a partner or candidate.

By accessing our website or utilizing our services, you acknowledge the terms described in this Privacy Policy. If you disagree with any portion of this policy, please discontinue use of our site and services.

2Information We Collect

We collect information across several categories to provide, optimize, and secure our services:

  • Information you provide directly — When you fill out contact forms, request proposals, subscribe to communications, or interact with our team, we collect identifiers such as your full name, professional email address, company name, telephone number, and the direct content of your message.
  • Project & Business Inquiries — To scope technical engagements, we gather project specifications, architecture requirements, technology stack preferences, timeline expectations, and budget details shared during discovery.
  • Automatically Collected Telemetry & Device Data — When you browse our website, our servers automatically log technical data, including your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, time spent on pages, and clickstream data.
  • Partner & candidate information — When onboarding contractors, vendors, or job applicants, we collect professional histories, skills matrices, availability schedules, identification details, and background verification data.

3How We Use It

We leverage collected data for specific, lawful business purposes, including to:

  • Respond to inquiries, provide technical proposals, and manage client onboarding.
  • Execute, deliver, maintain, and improve our software engineering, AI, cloud, and DevOps services.
  • Send transactional updates, administrative notifications, and—with explicit consent where required—marketing newsletters and campaign communications (you may opt out at any time).
  • Analyze website traffic, performance, and user behavior to optimize user experience and platform security.
  • Fulfill contractual commitments, resolve technical disputes, and comply with applicable local and international legal, tax, and regulatory obligations.
We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or client project datasets to third parties for marketing purposes.

4Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, understand site usage, and improve performance. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though some site features may not function properly as a result.

5Sharing & Disclosure

We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the site, process payments, or send communications, under confidentiality agreements
  • Authorities, where required by law, regulation, or legal process

6Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, satisfy tax and legal record-keeping requirements, and resolve disputes. You may request earlier deletion, subject to legal retention obligations.

7Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to opt out of marketing communications at any time via the unsubscribe link in our emails or by contacting us directly.

8Security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational safeguards, including encryption in transit and access controls, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised. Continued use of our site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
